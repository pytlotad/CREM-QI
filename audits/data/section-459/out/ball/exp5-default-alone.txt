T is the Kepler period of the osculating capture orbit, the longer of its capture-time and window-close values.
The revolution count is what the bound observation window actually covered, NOT a count to collapse: these pairs are
followed only long enough to classify them, so the window is bounded by boundObservationTimeCap.

so no orbit-averaged power exists for them.  These captures are loose enough that one period exceeds the bound observation cap.
